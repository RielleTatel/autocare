## What is AutoCare+?

AutoCare+ is a subscription-based car care app for Zamboanga City. Instead of only visiting a repair shop when something breaks, members pay a monthly membership and get ongoing, proactive care for their vehicle — inspections, maintenance reminders, pick-up and delivery, roadside help, and a simple health score that shows exactly how their car is doing at any time.

## The problem

Most car owners only think about maintenance when something has already gone wrong. Small issues go unnoticed until they turn into expensive repairs or a breakdown on the road. And when it's time to sell a car, there's usually no real proof of how well it was maintained — just the seller's word.

## The solution

AutoCare+ shifts car care from reactive to proactive. Members get:

- **Scheduled inspections and reminders**, so maintenance happens on time, before small problems become big ones
- **A Vehicle Health Score** — a simple number and star rating that sums up the car's condition, with plain-language explanations for every finding
- **A "What Needs Attention" home screen**, so members always know exactly what to do next, without digging through menus
- **Pick-up and delivery**, so getting a car serviced doesn't mean losing a day
- **Roadside assistance**, tracked live in the app, for breakdowns and emergencies
- **A digital service history** tied to the vehicle, replacing paper receipts and giving buyers real proof of maintenance at resale time

## Who it's for

- **Vehicle owners** in Zamboanga City who want convenient, predictable car care and peace of mind
- **Busy professionals and multi-car families** who value not having to personally drop off and pick up a vehicle
- **Fleet operators** (delivery fleets, jeepney cooperatives) who need multiple vehicles maintained reliably

## How members pay

Membership plans are priced monthly, with everything included clearly listed up front. Payment can be made in cash or through common e-wallets and cards (GCash, Maya, credit/debit) — whichever a member prefers.

## What makes this different

Traditional repair shops are reactive and transactional — a customer shows up once something is already wrong. AutoCare+ is a relationship: continuous care, a digital record that follows the vehicle, and a health score that turns "trust me, it's well maintained" into something a buyer can actually verify.
