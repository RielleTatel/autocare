The five-band VHS verdict as a pill. Use wherever a score needs its word — lists, headers, certificates.

```jsx
<BandChip score={69} showFil />
<BandChip band="CRITICAL" showRange />
```

Bands are protected product data: `EXCELLENT` 90–100, `GOOD` 75–89, `FAIR` 60–74, `NEEDS_ATTENTION` 40–59, `CRITICAL` 0–39. Exported helpers `bandForScore(score)` and `BANDS` let you colour anything else consistently. Never use these five colours for decoration.
