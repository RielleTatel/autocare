AutoCare+ Design System

One token set for four surfaces: the member iOS app, the field app for mechanics and drivers, the staff and admin web portal, and public Vehicle Health Score certificates. Workshop precision: calm steel neutrals, one confident blue, and score colors that are treated as data.

v1.0 · source of truth: packages/design-tokens · 2026-08-08

Principles

  • Band colors are product data, not decoration. The five VHS band colors appear only when they mean a score or an inspection status. Never use them as accents.
  • One bold element per screen. The score gauge, the SOS button, or the primary action — never all shouting at once.
  • Built for gloves and sunlight. The field app raises every requirement one notch: bigger targets, larger type, always-visible sync state.
  • Bilingual by design. Every label ships in English and Filipino; layouts allow ~30% text expansion.

Color

Neutrals are cool steel greys — a workshop, not a café. primary is Gauge Blue, the only marketing color. Semantic reds/greens for actions are distinct tokens from the band scale.

Protected tokens. Band fills are tuned to pass 3:1 with white text and to stay distinguishable for the most common color-vision deficiencies (the scale also always pairs color with a label and a number). Changing them requires a design review, and they are identical across iOS, web, and the printed certificate PDF.

Typography

Barlow Semi Condensed (600) for display — a face with road-signage DNA, used for headings and the score numeral. Inter for UI text (native apps use the system stack: SF Pro on iOS). IBM Plex Mono (500) for machine identity: plate numbers, VINs, receipt and verification codes. This page previews with system fallbacks; apps bundle the real faces.

Signature component — the score gauge

Honesty is rendered, not just computed

This vehicle averaged 84.5, but a safety-critical brake finding caps it at 69. The gauge always shows the capped score in the band color, with the top detractors directly beneath — the UI must always answer “why?”.

Core components

Buttons

Labels say exactly what happens: “Book a service”, not “Submit”. Destructive actions are red and always confirm (NFR-030). The tall variant is the field-app default (56 dp).

Status pills

DRAFT AWAITING APPROVAL IN PROGRESS PAST DUE PAID SUSPENDED

Every lifecycle in the product (work orders, invoices, trips, roadside) renders as mono-type pills. Blue = moving, green = settled, amber = needs someone, red = stopped.

Form field with error

This plate is already registered to another account. Check the number, or contact us if this is your vehicle.

Errors state what went wrong and what to do next — never raw codes (NFR-032). Machine identity (plates, VINs, codes) is always mono with letter-spacing.

Field app — offline sync banner

3 items waiting to sync

Persistent on every field screen whenever the outbox is non-empty. Staff must never wonder whether their work saved. Tapping opens the queue detail (screen F-03).

Voice

  • Plain verbs, sentence case, no jargon: “brake pads worn”, not “friction material below spec”.
  • An action keeps its name through the flow: “Approve work” → toast “Work approved”.
  • Not-yet-eligible states are non-punitive and offer the paid alternative (FR-035).
  • Filipino translations are first-class, reviewed, and shipped together with English (NFR-029).
